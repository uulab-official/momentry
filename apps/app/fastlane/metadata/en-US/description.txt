Momentry is a personal journal for everyday moments and the stories you discover in movies and books.

Write a diary entry, rate a movie while the feeling is still fresh, or save a reading note. As moments collect, your own memory tree grows.

Features
- Photo diary entries
- Movie discovery or manual entry, five-star ratings, and notes
- Book discovery or manual entry, five-star ratings, and reading notes
- System, light, and dark themes
- Local-first journaling with no account required

Entries are stored on your device by default.
