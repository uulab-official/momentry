No reviewer account is required. Momentry is a local-first personal journal and all user-created entries and selected photos remain in the app's local storage.

Suggested review path:
1. Open the Diary tab and create a diary entry with the floating add button.
2. Open the Movies tab, add a movie directly, give it a star rating, and save a short note.
3. Open the Books tab and add a reading note.
4. Open the More tab to review theme settings, FAQ, privacy policy, terms, and app information.

Photo Library permission is requested only when the reviewer explicitly chooses to attach a photo to an entry. The app does not require sign-in, contain purchases or subscriptions, display ads, or collect analytics. Movie and book search are optional conveniences; entries can always be created directly without a search provider.
